# One-Step Equations

## Overview

A **one-step equation** is an equation you can solve with exactly one operation applied to both sides. The goal is always the same: get the variable alone on one side. You do that by reversing whatever is being done to it.

## Key Idea

An equation is a balance — the left side equals the right side. The moment you perform an operation on one side, you must perform the exact same operation on the other side to keep that balance.

$$\text{If } x + a = b, \text{ then subtract } a \text{ from both sides: } x = b - a$$

$$\text{If } ax = b, \text{ then divide both sides by } a: x = \frac{b}{a}$$

The inverse operations are: addition undoes subtraction, subtraction undoes addition, multiplication undoes division, division undoes multiplication.

## Worked Examples

**Example 1: Solve $x + 9 = 14$**

The variable has 9 added to it. To undo addition, subtract. Subtracting 9 from the left side cancels the $+9$, isolating $x$. Subtracting 9 from the right side keeps the equation balanced.

$$x + 9 - 9 = 14 - 9 \implies x = 5$$

Check: $5 + 9 = 14$. Correct.

---

**Example 2: Solve $7x = 42$**

The variable is being multiplied by 7. To undo multiplication, divide. Dividing both sides by 7 leaves $x$ alone on the left. The right side, $42 \div 7$, simplifies to 6.

$$\frac{7x}{7} = \frac{42}{7} \implies x = 6$$

Check: $7 \cdot 6 = 42$. Correct.

---

**Example 3: Solve $x - 5 = -3$**

The variable has 5 subtracted from it. The inverse of subtraction is addition, so add 5 to both sides. On the left, $-5 + 5 = 0$, leaving $x$. On the right, $-3 + 5 = 2$.

$$x - 5 + 5 = -3 + 5 \implies x = 2$$

Check: $2 - 5 = -3$. Correct.

## Common Mistakes

- **Performing the operation on only one side.** If you subtract 9 from the left but forget the right, the equation is no longer balanced and your answer will be wrong.
- **Using the wrong inverse.** To undo $+7$, you subtract 7 — not multiply by 7. Match the operation type: addition/subtraction are inverses of each other, and multiplication/division are inverses of each other.
- **Skipping the check step.** Substituting your answer back into the original equation takes five seconds and immediately tells you if you made an arithmetic error.
