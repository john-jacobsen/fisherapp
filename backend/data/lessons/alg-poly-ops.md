# Polynomial Operations

## Overview

**Polynomial operations** — adding, subtracting, and multiplying polynomials — are the arithmetic of algebra. Adding and subtracting rely on combining like terms. Multiplying relies on the distributive property, applied systematically so every term in one polynomial multiplies every term in the other.

## Key Idea

For two binomials, the distributive property gives four products, remembered by FOIL:

$$(a + b)(c + d) = \underbrace{ac}_{\text{First}} + \underbrace{ad}_{\text{Outer}} + \underbrace{bc}_{\text{Inner}} + \underbrace{bd}_{\text{Last}}$$

For longer polynomials, distribute each term of the first polynomial across all terms of the second. Then combine like terms — terms with exactly the same variable and exponent.

## Worked Examples

**Example 1: Add $(3x^2 + 2x - 1) + (x^2 - 5x + 4)$**

Group like terms together:

$$(3x^2 + x^2) + (2x - 5x) + (-1 + 4) = 4x^2 - 3x + 3$$

The key is that only terms with the same variable and exponent can be combined. $3x^2$ and $x^2$ are like terms; $3x^2$ and $x$ are not.

---

**Example 2: Multiply $(x + 3)(x - 2)$**

Apply FOIL:

$$x \cdot x + x \cdot (-2) + 3 \cdot x + 3 \cdot (-2) = x^2 - 2x + 3x - 6 = x^2 + x - 6$$

The middle terms $-2x$ and $+3x$ are like terms and combine. The $x^2$ and $-6$ terms have nothing to combine with.

---

**Example 3: Multiply $(2x + 1)(3x^2 - x + 2)$**

Distribute $2x$ across the trinomial, then distribute $1$ across it:

$$2x(3x^2) + 2x(-x) + 2x(2) + 1(3x^2) + 1(-x) + 1(2)$$

$$= 6x^3 - 2x^2 + 4x + 3x^2 - x + 2$$

Combine like terms ($-2x^2$ and $+3x^2$; $+4x$ and $-x$):

$$= 6x^3 + x^2 + 3x + 2$$

## Common Mistakes

- **Adding exponents when combining like terms.** $3x^2 + x^2 = 4x^2$, not $4x^4$. You add the coefficients; the exponent does not change. Exponents only add when you multiply terms: $x^2 \cdot x^3 = x^5$.
- **Forgetting to distribute the negative when subtracting polynomials.** $(3x^2 + 2x) - (x^2 - 5x) = 3x^2 + 2x - x^2 + 5x = 2x^2 + 7x$. The subtraction applies to every term in the second polynomial, including the $-5x$ which becomes $+5x$.
- **Missing terms in FOIL by going out of order.** Use a systematic order — First, Outer, Inner, Last — to ensure all four products are included.
