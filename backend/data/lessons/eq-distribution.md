# Equations Requiring Distribution

## Overview

**Distribution** is the process of multiplying a factor outside parentheses by every term inside them. When an equation contains an expression like $3(x + 4)$, you cannot simply divide by 3 right away — the $x$ and the $4$ are grouped together, so dividing by 3 would only affect the whole group, not isolate $x$. Distributing first removes the grouping so you can work with each term separately.

## Key Idea

Apply the distributive property to eliminate parentheses, then solve:

$$a(b + c) = ab + ac$$

The full strategy: **distribute → combine like terms → isolate the variable.**

## Worked Examples

**Example 1: Solve $3(x + 4) = 21$**

You cannot divide both sides by 3 yet — that would give $x + 4 = 7$, which actually works here, but only because there is one group. Get into the habit of distributing first so the strategy works in all cases.

Distribute the 3 to both terms inside the parentheses:

$$3 \cdot x + 3 \cdot 4 = 21 \implies 3x + 12 = 21$$

Now subtract 12 from both sides to isolate the $x$ term — you are undoing the addition:

$$3x = 9$$

Divide both sides by 3 to undo the multiplication:

$$x = 3$$

---

**Example 2: Solve $2(x - 5) = 3x + 1$**

Distribute the 2 on the left. Notice the subtraction sign belongs to the $5$, so $2 \cdot (-5) = -10$:

$$2x - 10 = 3x + 1$$

Subtract $2x$ from both sides to gather the variable terms on the right — this keeps the coefficient positive, which reduces sign errors:

$$-10 = x + 1$$

Subtract 1 from both sides:

$$x = -11$$

You can verify: $2(-11 - 5) = 2(-16) = -32$ and $3(-11) + 1 = -33 + 1 = -32$. Both sides match.

---

**Example 3: Solve $4(2x + 1) - 3(x - 2) = 25$**

There are two groups to distribute. Handle each separately — pay close attention to the negative sign in front of the second group, which flips the sign of every term inside:

$$8x + 4 - 3x + 6 = 25$$

Note: $-3 \cdot (-2) = +6$, not $-6$. Now combine the like terms on the left:

$$5x + 10 = 25$$

Subtract 10, then divide by 5:

$$5x = 15 \implies x = 3$$

## Common Mistakes

- **Only distributing to the first term inside.** $3(x + 4) \ne 3x + 4$. The factor outside multiplies every term inside, so $3(x + 4) = 3x + 12$.
- **Dropping the negative when distributing a negative factor.** $-2(x - 3) = -2x + 6$, not $-2x - 6$. The negative times a negative gives a positive.
- **Combining terms across the equals sign before moving them.** Only combine like terms that are already on the same side.
