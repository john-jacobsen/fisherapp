    "old" and "new" values, so SQLAlchemy detected no change → no UPDATE emitted
    → ephemeral_problems lost after commit 1 → "Problem not found" on submit 3.
Fix:
  - import copy; state = copy.deepcopy(session.state_snapshot)
  - flag_modified(session, "state_snapshot") before every db.commit()
